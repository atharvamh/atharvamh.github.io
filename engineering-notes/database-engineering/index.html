Database Engineering

Notes on how a database actually works underneath the SQL — transactions, storage, indexing, and the tradeoffs engines make to keep both fast and correct.

I

ACID, Transactions & Concurrency

How a database keeps its promises when things crash, and how it keeps those promises without making every transaction wait in line — ACID guarantees, durability & recovery, isolation levels, MVCC internals, and the read anomalies that surface when transactions run side by side.

 II

Storage Internals & Indexing

How a table's rows actually sit on disk, why B+Trees won out over every other search tree, and why a single choice of primary key can make or break write throughput — pages, heaps, slotted page layout, clustered vs. heap tables, and row-oriented vs. columnar storage.
