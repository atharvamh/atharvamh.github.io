Attack on Titan: Final Episodes Summary

Timeline of Eren's Actions

  1. The Vision of the Rumbling
    • After touching Historia's hand during the Scout Regiment’s ceremony, Eren sees the memories of the future.
    • He sees the devastation of the Rumbling and realizes his predetermined path.
  1. A Final Chance in Marley
    • Eren privately asks Mikasa: “What am I to you?”
    • He hopes her answer will give him a reason to escape his fate.
    • Mikasa replies that he is “family”, confirming for Eren that he cannot avoid his destiny.
  1. The Last Conversation with Armin
    • Through the Paths, Eren reveals his true plan to Armin.
    • He admits his inner conflict, selfish feelings, and deep affection for Mikasa.
    • Eren erases Armin’s memory of this talk, which only returns after Eren’s death.
  1. The Final Vision for Mikasa
    • Just before his death, Eren shows Mikasa an alternate life where they ran away together.
    • He confesses his true feelings and wishes for her to remember him.

Why Did Mikasa Kill Eren?

  • Not giving up love, but choosing freely
    • Her choice was not rejection but proof that her love was conscious, not forced.
  • Rejecting the fantasy
    • She chose reality over Eren’s “cabin dream”, showing true free will.
  • Freeing them both
    • Her action freed Eren from his destructive destiny.
    • She also freed herself from living only for him.

How Did Mikasa’s Action Free Ymir?

  • The Parallel
    • Ymir was enslaved by her love for King Fritz.
    • Mikasa also loved Eren deeply but broke free by ending his violence.
  • A New Perspective for Ymir
    • Ymir saw that love does not equal submission.
    • Mikasa’s choice showed her that bonds can be broken.
  • The Final Kiss
    • Mikasa beheaded Eren and kissed him goodbye.
    • This act of free love and choice allowed Ymir to finally find peace and end the Curse of the Titans.

Symbolism & Easter Eggs

  • The Bird and the Scarf
    • After Eren’s death, a bird (seen as his spirit or will within nature) visits Mikasa.
    • It tugs on her red scarf — a symbol of their bond since childhood.
    • This represents Eren’s final wish for Mikasa to be free from him.
  • Eren’s “Invisible Hand” Across Time
    • By accessing the Founding Titan’s power, Eren influenced past, present, and future.
    • He guided key moments, such as Dina Fritz (the Smiling Titan) eating Carla instead of Bertholdt.
    • This shows his actions shaped events from the very beginning.
  • Armin as Humanity’s Hero
    • After Eren’s death, Armin negotiates peace with Marley and other nations.
    • Eren’s sacrifice makes Armin the one who can lead humanity toward peace.
  • Ymir’s Smile
    • Before fading, Ymir smiles after witnessing Mikasa’s choice.
    • This symbolizes her release from millennia of slavery to King Fritz.
  • Eren’s True Motive for the Rumbling
    • Eren reveals to Armin that he didn’t want to destroy the world just out of hate.
    • His real goal was to make himself the ultimate enemy so his friends could be remembered as heroes who saved humanity.
    • This “martyr complex” adds another layer to his sacrifice.
  • Armin Thanking Eren in the Paths
    • In their final conversation, Armin thanks Eren for becoming the villain so the rest of them could live.
    • This shows Armin’s understanding of Eren’s burden, and it softens the tragedy of their friendship.
  • Founding Power Ends With Eren’s Death
    • Once Eren dies and Ymir is freed, the power of the Titans disappears completely.
    • All Eldians return to being normal humans (no more shifters, no more Titans at all).
    • This is the definitive end of the Titan curse.
  • Mikasa Carrying Eren’s Head
    • After killing Eren, Mikasa carries his head in her arms.
    • This mirrors ancient imagery of lovers in tragedy, and underlines her devotion even in the act of letting him go.
  • Humanity’s Fragile Peace
    • The closing sequence after the time skip shows Paradis modernizing but still being attacked and eventually destroyed.
    • This reflects the central theme: hatred and war will always resurface, even without Titans.
  • The Final Grave Scene
    • Mikasa visits Eren’s grave under the same tree from episode 1, where he once napped.
    • The tree becomes a full-circle motif, symbolizing both Eren’s beginning and end.

Ymir's Alternate Choice (Spear Scene)

  • In the final montage, we briefly see an alternate image of Ymir not saving King Fritz from the spear.
  • Instead of jumping in front of him (which is what really happened), the spear pierces and kills him.
  • Ymir stands holding her children — alive, but without being bound to Fritz's will.
  • Meaning: This suggests that if Ymir had chosen differently in that critical moment 2,000 years ago, the Titan curse would never have begun. She would have lived free instead of becoming a slave to King Fritz's lineage.
  • The scene shows what could have been — a mirror to Mikasa's own choice to break free from Eren.
