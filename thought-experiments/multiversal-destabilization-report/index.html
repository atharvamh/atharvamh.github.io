Archive Summary

Even after the Infinity Stones were returned and the timeline was, in theory, made whole, Earth‑616 never fully closed its wounds. A death at the end of time. A memory spell gone wrong. Chaos magic left to root. Each incident on its own was contained — survivable. Together, they are eight cracks in the same pane of glass. What follows is the case‑by‑case record of how one universe's shield came down.
