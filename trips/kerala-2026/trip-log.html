🌴 Wildlife Week — Oct 2 to Oct 8

Kerala's national parks, tiger reserves, and sanctuaries run free or exempted entry fees for Wildlife Week celebrations across multiple locations during this window. Worth asking at the counter — could apply to Periyar, Eravikulam, and others on this list.

10

Anayirankal Dam

📍 ~22 km from Munnar, on the Munnar-Thekkady road

Better than Mattupetty Dam. One of Kerala's largest earthen dams, built in the 1960s as part of the Panniyar Hydroelectric Project — the name translates to "where elephants come down to drink," and elephants, gaur, and deer are still sometimes spotted on the banks.

Thekkady, Kumily Town
11

Kalaripayattu Show, Navarasa Centre

📍 Kumily Thekkady Road, Kumily town

An excellent performance, 6–7 PM. Kalaripayattu is one of the world's oldest martial art forms (roughly 3,000 years old), drawing its movements from animals — lion, tiger, elephant, snake, crocodile. The show includes sword and fire sequences, a narrator explaining each move in English, and a photo opportunity with the performers afterward. Ranked #1 of 36 Tours & Activities in Thekkady on Tripadvisor, 4.4★ across 1,000+ reviews.

12

Spice Shopping, Kumily Market

📍 Kumily town

Picked up spices from the local market — cardamom, pepper, cinnamon, and more. Regular market prices here beat the tour-attached spice garden shops by a wide margin.

13

Periyar Tiger Reserve

Got entry + bus ticket at 6 AM, bus departed 6:30 AM; 7:30 AM boating slot already sold out (slots very limited), so walked the grounds instead. Felt highly commercialized — not worth visiting without a pre-booked activity.

Thekkady to Alleppey (via Kottayam)
14

Valanjanganam Waterfalls

📍 On the highway near Kuttikkanam, Idukki district

Quick stop — very refreshing vibe. Also known as Ninnumullippara, about 75 ft in height and usually mist-covered. Right on the road, so no detour needed — a natural break on a long drive.

15

Pineapple Farms

📍 Ghat road near Kanjirapally, Kottayam district

Seen along the way. Kerala's main pineapple belt is actually Ernakulam district (Vazhakulam) further north, but Kottayam district — including this Kanjirapally stretch — also grows it commercially.

16

Rubber Farms — Pazhoothadam Estate

📍 Parathode, Kanjirapally, Kottayam district

Seen on the way. Kottayam district has a genuine rubber-growing history — it's one of the earliest places in India where rubber was commercially cultivated.

Chilling at the resort

Dinner at Havana Restaurant

Headed out from the resort for this — amazing, tasty food. Ordered the Kareemen Fish Curry, Kalinji Fish Curry, Cheemen (Prawns), and the Kalinji Fish Fry — the fry came out as a whole fish, massive portion. Total bill for 4 people: just ₹2,450.

20

Shikara Ride to Pathiramanal Island

📍 Open Vembanad Lake, Muhamma panchayat

7–8 AM on Oct 6 morning — a nice, serene ride across the open lake, followed by a stroll through the island. Back at the resort by 8:05 AM. Pathiramanal (meaning "Sands of Midnight") is genuinely a migratory bird spot — home to roughly 90 local species and 50 migratory species, including visitors from as far as Siberia and Europe. The island is uninhabited and largely undisturbed, so it functions as a quiet roosting ground. Peak migratory season is Nov–Feb, so an October visit is right at the early edge of it — still a good chance of sightings, if not quite peak.

21

Alleppey Lighthouse

📍 Opposite Alleppey Beach

Checked out of the resort at 11 AM and headed here (open 8 AM–12 PM, 2 PM–5:30 PM) — climbed up for a view of Alleppey Beach and the old pier ruins below. Built in 1862, one of the oldest lighthouses on India's southwest coast, 30 m tall with a 100+ step wooden spiral staircase. It originally ran on a coconut-oil double-wick lamp (used until 1952) and only opened to the public in 2007. The pier below is around 137–150 years old and now in ruins — a relic of Alleppey's days as a major colonial-era trading port.
